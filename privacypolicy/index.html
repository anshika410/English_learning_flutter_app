Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

Ella is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and process your personal data in connection with the Ella English learning application.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect voice recordings, names, and ages to customize the conversational learning experience and place learners at the appropriate CEFR level.

2. How We Use Information

Your data is used solely to improve your speaking skills, run AI conversation simulations, and track your learning progress.

3. Data Deletion

You can request data deletion at any time via the profile settings inside the app.
