Privacy Policy - Ella

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Ella is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, process, and protect your personal data in connection with the Ella English learning application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a personalized learning experience, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account & Profile Data: We collect your name, age, and email address.
  • Voice & Conversation Data: We collect voice recordings when you interact with the app. These recordings are transcribed into text to facilitate conversation.
  • Device & Usage Data: We track your learning progress, CEFR level, and interactions within the app to tailor the curriculum to your needs.

2. How We Use Information

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Ella learning experience:

  • Conversational AI: Your voice transcripts are processed by Large Language Models (AI) to simulate real-time conversations, grade your language proficiency, and provide feedback.
  • Identity Protection: While we securely store your full name for account management purposes, we restrict our AI services and the application interface to strictly use only your first name. This ensures your personal identity is minimized during AI conversation simulations and throughout your learning experience.
  • Personalization: We use your age and learning history to curate relevant conversation topics and adapt the difficulty of the curriculum.

3. Third-Party Services

To power our conversation features, we use third-party AI providers to process text transcripts. We do not share your full name or personal contact information with these AI providers. Your transcripts are sent securely and are used solely for generating conversational responses and language assessments.

4. Data Security and Deletion

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information. You have the right to control your data:

  • You can request complete data deletion at any time via the profile settings inside the app. Upon request, we will permanently delete your account, profile data, and conversation history.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy or your data, please contact our support team through the application.
