This document outlines the structured pedagogical framework of Zoe. Each level consists of 5 progressive units, with specific Vocabulary (VOC), Grammar (GRA), and Fluency (FLU) targets.

## Level A0
*I can talk about myself and things around me.*

### Step 1: First words
**Focus:** Naming the things I can see.

**Targeted Skills:**
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U1-VOC-01</span> (Vocabulary): I can name colours and say numbers 1–10.
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U1-VOC-02</span> (Vocabulary): I can name familiar animals and everyday objects at school and home.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U1-GRA-01</span> (Grammar): I can use 'a' and 'the' in a simple phrase — a cat, the bag.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U1-FLU-01</span> (Fluency): I can name things in English when someone points at them.

### Step 2: I am and I have got
**Focus:** Saying who I am and what I have.

**Targeted Skills:**
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U2-VOC-01</span> (Vocabulary): I can say my name, my age, and the name of my school and family members.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U2-GRA-01</span> (Grammar): I can say 'I am' and 'I am not' — I am happy. I am not tired.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U2-GRA-02</span> (Grammar): I can use 'I have got' — I have got a dog. I haven't got a sister.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U2-FLU-01</span> (Fluency): I can introduce myself in English in a few short phrases.

### Step 3: I like and I don't like
**Focus:** Saying what I enjoy — and what I don't.

**Targeted Skills:**
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U3-VOC-01</span> (Vocabulary): I can name things I like — food, sports, animals, colours.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U3-GRA-01</span> (Grammar): I can say 'I like' and 'I don't like' — I like cricket. I don't like eggs.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U3-GRA-02</span> (Grammar): I can use 'and' to join two things — I like cats and dogs.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U3-FLU-01</span> (Fluency): I can answer 'Do you like...?' with a short sentence, not just yes or no.

### Step 4: I can
**Focus:** Saying what I am able to do.

**Targeted Skills:**
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U4-VOC-01</span> (Vocabulary): I can name positions — on, in, under, next to, behind.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U4-GRA-01</span> (Grammar): I can use 'can' to say what someone is able to do — I can run. She can't fly.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U4-GRA-02</span> (Grammar): I can say where things are — The bag is on the chair. There is a pen on the table.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U4-FLU-01</span> (Fluency): I can answer 'What can you do?' with a simple sentence.

### Step 5: What is happening?
**Focus:** Using present continuous and putting it all together in a short exchange.

**Targeted Skills:**
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U5-GRA-01</span> (Grammar): I can use present continuous for right now — She is running. They are eating.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U5-GRA-02</span> (Grammar): I can answer questions in the present — What are you doing? I am reading.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U5-FLU-01</span> (Fluency): I can keep a simple exchange going for 2–3 turns on a familiar topic.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U5-FLU-02</span> (Fluency): I can keep going even when I only have a few words.

## Level A1
*I can have simple conversations on everyday topics.*

### Step 1: Describing the world
**Focus:** Moving from naming things to describing them.

**Targeted Skills:**
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U1-VOC-01</span> (Vocabulary): I can describe what people look like and how they feel using simple adjectives.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U1-GRA-01</span> (Grammar): I can use the present continuous to describe what is happening now — She is running.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U1-FLU-01</span> (Fluency): I can describe a picture or a place using 3–4 connected sentences.

### Step 2: Talking about the past
**Focus:** Starting to use past tense to share what happened.

**Targeted Skills:**
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U2-VOC-01</span> (Vocabulary): I can use past time expressions — yesterday, last week, a long time ago.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U2-GRA-01</span> (Grammar): I can use past simple with regular verbs — I walked, I watched, I played.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U2-GRA-02</span> (Grammar): I can use some irregular past verbs — I went, I saw, I ate, I had.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U2-FLU-01</span> (Fluency): I can talk about something I did recently in a few connected sentences.

### Step 3: Giving opinions and reasons
**Focus:** Beginning to say what I think and why.

**Targeted Skills:**
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U3-VOC-01</span> (Vocabulary): I can use simple opinion words — I think, I feel, I prefer, I love, I hate.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U3-GRA-01</span> (Grammar): I can use 'because' to give a reason — I like cricket because it's exciting.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U3-GRA-02</span> (Grammar): I can use 'but' to show contrast — I like football but not swimming.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U3-FLU-01</span> (Fluency): I can share a simple opinion and give at least one reason for it.

### Step 4: Talking about plans
**Focus:** Extending into the future — making and sharing plans.

**Targeted Skills:**
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U4-VOC-01</span> (Vocabulary): I can use frequency words — always, usually, sometimes, never, often.
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U4-VOC-02</span> (Vocabulary): I can use future time expressions — tomorrow, next week, soon, one day.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U4-GRA-01</span> (Grammar): I can use 'going to' to talk about a plan — I'm going to study tonight.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U4-FLU-01</span> (Fluency): I can talk about what I am going to do and give a simple reason why.

### Step 5: Holding a real conversation
**Focus:** Using everything together in a real back-and-forth.

**Targeted Skills:**
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U5-VOC-01</span> (Vocabulary): I can use connecting phrases — first, then, after that, in the end.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U5-GRA-01</span> (Grammar): I can use present simple, present continuous, and past simple together in a story.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U5-FLU-01</span> (Fluency): I can hold a short back-and-forth conversation on a familiar topic without long pauses.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U5-FLU-02</span> (Fluency): I can keep going even when I'm not 100% sure of every word.

## Level A2
*I can share my opinions and tell stories in English.*

### Step 1: Finding better words
**Focus:** Moving from words that work to words that feel right.

**Targeted Skills:**
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U1-VOC-01</span> (Vocabulary): I can use a wider range of descriptive words to make my ideas more interesting.
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U1-VOC-02</span> (Vocabulary): I can understand and use common idioms I hear regularly.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U1-GRA-01</span> (Grammar): I can use present perfect to connect past and present — I've lived here for 3 years.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U1-FLU-01</span> (Fluency): I can express an idea in more than one way when I'm not sure of a word.

### Step 2: Talking about the past with more precision
**Focus:** Describing not just what happened, but what was happening and what used to be.

**Targeted Skills:**
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U2-VOC-01</span> (Vocabulary): I can use sequence and contrast words — however, although, even though.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U2-GRA-01</span> (Grammar): I can use past continuous with past simple — I was eating when she called.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U2-GRA-02</span> (Grammar): I can use 'used to' to talk about past habits.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U2-FLU-01</span> (Fluency): I can tell a story that moves naturally between past and present.

### Step 3: Expressing opinions with depth
**Focus:** Moving from simple opinions to supported arguments.

**Targeted Skills:**
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U3-VOC-01</span> (Vocabulary): I can use reporting words — I think, I believe, in my opinion, it seems to me.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U3-GRA-01</span> (Grammar): I can use first conditional — If I study hard, I will pass.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U3-GRA-02</span> (Grammar): I can use modal verbs — might, could, should, must — with reasonable accuracy.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U3-FLU-01</span> (Fluency): I can give an opinion, support it with a reason, and add an example.

### Step 4: Speaking for longer without losing the thread
**Focus:** Building up to a full minute of organised, connected speech.

**Targeted Skills:**
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U4-VOC-01</span> (Vocabulary): I can use linking adverbials — as a result, in addition, on the other hand.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U4-GRA-01</span> (Grammar): I can use relative clauses to add detail — the person who, the place where.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U4-FLU-01</span> (Fluency): I can speak for about 1 minute on a familiar topic without stopping.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U4-FLU-02</span> (Fluency): I can organise what I say so others can follow my thinking easily.

### Step 5: Handling unpredictable conversations
**Focus:** Communicating flexibly in varied situations.

**Targeted Skills:**
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U5-VOC-01</span> (Vocabulary): I can use formal and informal vocabulary depending on who I'm talking to.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U5-GRA-01</span> (Grammar): I can use a range of tenses together accurately in an extended story or explanation.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U5-FLU-01</span> (Fluency): I can switch between topics in a conversation without losing the thread.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U5-FLU-02</span> (Fluency): I feel comfortable joining a conversation I didn't prepare for.

## Level B1
*I can speak for about a minute on many different topics.*

### Step 1: Precision in vocabulary
**Focus:** Choosing more exact and sophisticated words.

**Targeted Skills:**
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U1-VOC-01</span> (Vocabulary): I can use a wider range of vocabulary including less common and more precise words.
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U1-VOC-02</span> (Vocabulary): I can use collocations naturally — make a decision, pay attention, take a risk.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U1-GRA-01</span> (Grammar): I can use second conditional fluently — If I had more time, I would travel more.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U1-FLU-01</span> (Fluency): I can express a complex idea clearly without simplifying it too much.

### Step 2: Using complex grammar naturally
**Focus:** Using third conditional, reported speech, and hedging language with confidence.

**Targeted Skills:**
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U2-VOC-01</span> (Vocabulary): I can use hedging language — it could be argued, there is some evidence that.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U2-GRA-01</span> (Grammar): I can use third conditional — If I had studied harder, I would have passed.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U2-GRA-02</span> (Grammar): I can use reported speech fluidly — She said she would come. He asked if I was okay.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U2-FLU-01</span> (Fluency): I can use complex sentences without losing the main point.

### Step 3: Speaking at length with coherence
**Focus:** Building extended, well-organised spoken responses.

**Targeted Skills:**
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U3-VOC-01</span> (Vocabulary): I can use discourse markers to organise speech — right, so, as I was saying, anyway.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U3-GRA-01</span> (Grammar): I can use passive and active voice flexibly depending on what I want to emphasise.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U3-FLU-01</span> (Fluency): I can speak for 3–5 minutes on a topic I care about with clear structure.
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U3-FLU-02</span> (Fluency): I can keep a conversation coherent even when the topic shifts.

### Step 4: Arguing and persuading
**Focus:** Moving from sharing opinions to defending and challenging ideas.

**Targeted Skills:**
- <span className="bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-900/40 dark:text-blue-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U4-VOC-01</span> (Vocabulary): I can use vocabulary for argumentation — claim, evidence, argue, counter, support.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U4-GRA-01</span> (Grammar): I can use a range of linking devices to build structured arguments.
- <span className="bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-900/40 dark:text-emerald-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U4-GRA-02</span> (Grammar): I can use cleft sentences for emphasis — What I mean is… It was she who…
- <span className="bg-purple-50 text-purple-700 dark:bg-purple-900/40 dark:text-purple-300 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded text-xs font-bold font-mono mr-1">U4-FLU-01</span> (Fluency): I can challenge someone's opinion respectfully and explain my own position clearly.

### Step 5: Natural, spontaneous fluency
**Focus:** Speaking without noticeable effort on a wide range of topics.

**Targeted Skills:**
