01

Buttons

All buttons use Shape B asymmetric corners (4px 20px 4px 20px) with press-depth shadow. The shadow collapses on press, not a translate-only animation. Ghost/secondary mirrors the corner diagonal.

Flutter

ElevatedButton with custom ButtonStyle. Use BorderRadius.only(topLeft: 4, topRight: 20, bottomRight: 4, bottomLeft: 20). Ghost button inverts. Press animation via GestureDetector + AnimatedContainer on BoxDecoration.boxShadow.

Don't

Never use --ink as the button shadow colour. Shadow must always be the color-dark token of that button's family. Ink is reserved for avatar ring only. Do not mix shapes — all buttons use Shape B.

02

Ella Speech Bubble

Ella's voice is always Kalam — the handwriting font that supports Devanagari. The bubble attaches visually to the avatar via a small triangle nub. Never use a chat-bubble tail; the nub is directional, not conversational.

Do

Always lead with Kalam for Ella's first sentence. The greeting line sets her personality. Body content after the greeting can use Baloo 2 Bold for readability at scale.

Don't

Don't use Caveat. It has no Devanagari support — any Hindi mid-sentence causes a visible font fallback. Kalam was designed by Indian Type Foundry with screen rendering in mind.

03

User Response Chips

Chips are permanent scaffolding — not training wheels to be removed at higher levels. At A1 they carry full phrases. At B1+ they carry partial phrases or prompts. They use press-depth shadow like buttons but at reduced weight (3px → 1px).

Pedagogy

Chips are permanent scaffolding. At A1 they carry the entire phrase. At B1+ they become sentence starters. At B2 they become abstract prompts. The chip itself never disappears — its content evolves with the student's level.

Flutter

Use ActionChip or custom GestureDetector wrapping a Container. The inset border + bottom shadow combination requires stacking two BoxDecoration shadows with a border property. Use AnimatedContainer for state transitions.

System rule

The ink border on the avatar is one of only two reserved uses of --ink in the system. The other is the CTA button's shadow token. Everything else uses colour-family tokens. This restriction preserves the weight and meaning of ink.

Flutter

Pulse rings are AnimatedContainer with BoxDecoration(shape: BoxShape.circle) and an animated boxShadow spread radius. Use AnimationController with repeat() for speaking/listening, and forward() once for success burst.
