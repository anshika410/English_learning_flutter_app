# Ella · A1→A2 Skill Labels & Icons

**Design Reference · NavGurukul · June 2026**

Revised student-facing labels for all 19 skills across the 5 units of the A1→A2 path.
Original "I can" statements included for review and handoff.

**Track key:** 🌸 Vocabulary · 🔵 Grammar · 🟣 Fluency

---

## Unit 1 of 5 — Describing the World

*Go from naming things to describing them — people, places, and what's happening around you.*

| Icon | New Label | Track | Original "I can" statement |
|------|-----------|-------|---------------------------|
| 👫 | Describe people and how they feel | 🌸 Vocabulary | I can describe what people look like and how they feel using simple adjectives. |
| 🏃 | Talk about what's happening right now | 🔵 Grammar | I can use the present continuous to describe what is happening now — She is running. |
| 🏞️ | Describe a place or a picture | 🟣 Fluency | I can describe a picture or a place using 3–4 connected sentences. |

---

## Unit 2 of 5 — Talking About the Past

*Start placing yourself in time — talk about what happened yesterday, last week, or before.*

| Icon | New Label | Track | Original "I can" statement |
|------|-----------|-------|---------------------------|
| 📅 | Use words like yesterday, last week | 🌸 Vocabulary | I can use past time expressions — yesterday, last week, a long time ago. |
| 👟 | Use walked, played, watched correctly | 🔵 Grammar | I can use past simple with regular verbs — I walked, I watched, I played. |
| 🔀 | Use tricky past words like went, saw | 🔵 Grammar | I can use some irregular past verbs — I went, I saw, I ate, I had. |
| 📖 | Tell a short story about your day | 🟣 Fluency | I can talk about something I did recently in a few connected sentences. |

---

## Unit 3 of 5 — Giving Opinions and Reasons

*Say what you think, not just what you see — share opinions and give a reason for them.*

| Icon | New Label | Track | Original "I can" statement |
|------|-----------|-------|---------------------------|
| 💭 | Use opinion words like I think, I feel | 🌸 Vocabulary | I can use simple opinion words — I think, I feel, I prefer, I love, I hate. |
| 🔗 | Give a reason using because | 🔵 Grammar | I can use "because" to give a reason — I like cricket because it's exciting. |
| ⚖️ | Show contrast using but | 🔵 Grammar | I can use "but" to show contrast — I like football but not swimming. |
| 🗣️ | Share your opinion and explain why | 🟣 Fluency | I can share a simple opinion and give at least one reason for it. |

---

## Unit 4 of 5 — Talking About Plans

*Talk about what comes next — your habits, your intentions, and what you're going to do.*

| Icon | New Label | Track | Original "I can" statement |
|------|-----------|-------|---------------------------|
| 🔄 | Use words like always, sometimes, never | 🌸 Vocabulary | I can use frequency words — always, usually, sometimes, never, often. |
| ⏩ | Use words like tomorrow, next week | 🌸 Vocabulary | I can use future time expressions — tomorrow, next week, soon, one day. |
| 🎯 | Say what you're going to do | 🔵 Grammar | I can use "going to" to talk about a plan — I'm going to study tonight. |
| 🗓️ | Talk about your plans and why | 🟣 Fluency | I can talk about what I am going to do and give a simple reason why. |

---

## Unit 5 of 5 — Holding a Real Conversation

*Put it all together — mix tenses, connect ideas, and keep a real back-and-forth going.*

| Icon | New Label | Track | Original "I can" statement |
|------|-----------|-------|---------------------------|
| 📝 | Connect ideas with first, then, after that | 🌸 Vocabulary | I can use connecting phrases — first, then, after that, in the end. |
| 🕐 | Mix past, present, and future together | 🔵 Grammar | I can use present simple, present continuous, and past simple together in a story. |
| 💬 | Keep a conversation going without pauses | 🟣 Fluency | I can hold a short back-and-forth conversation on a familiar topic without long pauses. |
| 💪 | Keep speaking even when you're unsure | 🟣 Fluency | I can keep going even when I'm not 100% sure of every word. |

## Open Items for Review

- **Prachi to confirm:** "Use walked, played, watched **correctly**" — the word *correctly* may carry deficit framing. Alternative: *"Use walked, played, watched in a sentence."*
- **Sael to confirm:** All icons above are emoji placeholders. Abstract icons (🔀 🕐 ⚖️) may need custom illustrated versions or replacements.
- **Unit 5, skill 17:** 🕐 (clock) for "Mix past, present, and future together" is the weakest icon mapping — open to alternatives.
