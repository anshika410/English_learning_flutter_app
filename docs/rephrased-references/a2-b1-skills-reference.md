# Ella · A2→B1 Skill Labels & Icons

**Design Reference · NavGurukul · June 2026**

Revised student-facing labels for all 20 skills across the 5 units of the A2→B1 path.
Original "I can" statements included for review and handoff.

**Track key:** 🌸 Vocabulary · 🔵 Grammar · 🟣 Fluency

---

## Unit 1 of 5 — Finding Better Words

*Move from words that work to words that feel exactly right.*

| Icon | New Label | Track | Original "I can" statement |
|------|-----------|-------|---------------------------|
| 🎨 | Choose words that are richer and more exact | 🌸 Vocabulary | I can use a wider range of descriptive words to make my ideas more interesting. |
| 🗝️ | Use everyday idioms the way they're meant | 🌸 Vocabulary | I can understand and use common idioms I hear regularly. |
| ⏳ | Talk about what has happened up to now | 🔵 Grammar | I can use present perfect to connect past and present — I've lived here for 3 years. |

## Unit 2 of 5 — Talking About the Past with More Precision

*Add precision to the past — describe what was happening, not just what happened.*

| Icon | New Label | Track | Original "I can" statement |
|------|-----------|-------|---------------------------|
| ⚡ | Use contrast words like however and although | 🌸 Vocabulary | I can use sequence and contrast words — however, although, even though. |
| 🎬 | Describe what was happening when something else happened | 🔵 Grammar | I can use past continuous with past simple — I was eating when she called. |
| ⏮️ | Talk about what you used to do | 🔵 Grammar | I can use 'used to' to talk about past habits. |
| 🎞️ | Tell a story switching between past and present | 🟣 Fluency | I can tell a story that moves naturally between past and present. |

## Unit 3 of 5 — Expressing Opinions with Depth

*Build an argument — opinion, reason, and example, all together.*

| Icon | New Label | Track | Original "I can" statement |
|------|-----------|-------|---------------------------|
| 💬 | Frame opinions with I believe, in my opinion | 🌸 Vocabulary | I can use reporting words — I think, I believe, in my opinion, it seems to me. |
| 🌿 | Predict what will happen using if and will | 🔵 Grammar | I can use first conditional — If I study hard, I will pass. |
| 🎲 | Use might, could, should to add more meaning | 🔵 Grammar | I can use modal verbs — might, could, should, must — with reasonable accuracy. |
| 🏗️ | Give an opinion with a reason and example | 🟣 Fluency | I can give an opinion, support it with a reason, and add an example. |

## Unit 4 of 5 — Speaking for Longer Without Losing the Thread

*Learn to hold the floor — speak longer and keep listeners with you.*

| Icon | New Label | Track | Original "I can" statement |
|------|-----------|-------|---------------------------|
| 🔗 | Link ideas with as a result, in addition | 🌸 Vocabulary | I can use linking adverbials — as a result, in addition, on the other hand. |
| 🔍 | Add detail using who, which, where, that | 🔵 Grammar | I can use relative clauses to add detail — the person who, the place where. |

## Open Items for Review

- **Prachi — Unit 1, Grammar:** "Talk about what has happened up to now" hides the term *present perfect* completely. Confirm whether the label should hint at the structure (e.g. "Use I've done, I've seen to connect past and now") or stay fully example-free.
- **Prachi — Unit 3, Grammar (first conditional):** "Predict what will happen using if and will" removes the term *first conditional*. Confirm this is right for A2 students — or if a structural hint helps.
- **Sael — Unit 1, Fluency:** ↔️ (two-way arrow) for paraphrasing is abstract. Could also be 🔄 or custom illustrated. Flag for review.
- **Sael — Unit 3, Grammar (modals):** 🎲 (dice) works conceptually (possibility/uncertainty) but reads differently across cultures. Alternative: 🤔 or a custom illustration.
