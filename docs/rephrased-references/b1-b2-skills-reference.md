## Open Items for Review

- **Prachi — Unit 2, Vocabulary:** "Soften a claim without backing away from it" is a fairly abstract description of hedging. Confirm whether B1 students understand what "a claim" means, or if rephrasing to "Qualify what you say without fully backing down" is clearer.
- **Prachi — Unit 4, Grammar:** "cleft sentences" is kept in the label as this is the one place grammar terminology appears — at B1→B2 level students are likely familiar with the term. Confirm this is appropriate, or replace with: "Highlight what matters most — What I mean is... / It was..."
- **Prachi — Unit 5, Grammar:** "Control complex grammar with confidence and ease" removes the "B2 grammar" reference. Confirm this is specific enough, or if the label needs to gesture at the range of structures involved.
- **Sael — Unit 2, Vocabulary:** 🌫️ (fog) for hedging language is abstract. Could be 🛡️ or a custom softening illustration. Flag for review.
- **Sael — Unit 4, Vocabulary:** ⚔️ (crossed swords) for argumentation vocabulary — confirm this reads as "debate" not "conflict" in the visual context of the app.
- **Sael — Unit 5, Fluency:** 🔑 for "English is truly your own" — this is a metaphor, not a literal key. May need a custom illustration to land correctly.
